Cozy Cafe+
==========

Cozy Cafe+ is a Fabric fork of Cozy Cafe by Nitbe and Chakyl:
https://github.com/Chakyl/Cozy-Cafe

The original mod (Forge, version 1.13) is licensed under the GNU General Public
License v3.0, and so is this fork (see LICENSE.txt).

Taken from the original
-----------------------
- The idea: tables with menus, serving plates, NPC customers who come to eat.
- Block and item models, most textures and the clipboard-style GUI art.
- Parts of the code (block entities, the customer entity, renderers), ported from
  Forge to Fabric.

Changed and added in Cozy Cafe+ (by bloeiende)
---------------------------------------------
- Ported to Fabric 1.20.1.
- A cafe is a Privit claim: one Cafe Manager per claim; owner, waiters and the
  waiter's apron; operators can step in.
- A menu of any items with prices in a currency the owner picks; settings,
  cash register and debtors tabs; the cafe rating.
- Player guests: ordering, serving in parts, cancelling, timers, the bill with
  payment and a 1-5 star rating, unpaid meals becoming debts.
- NPC guests walk like villagers and open the cafe's doors. They come by day,
  only while staff is on site, more often at higher ratings, and pay by how
  filling the served food was.
- Shift report for the owner, the OPEN / CLOSED sign, waypoints for Xaero's
  Minimap, the GUI reworked, English and Russian text.
- GameTests covering the cafe's rules.

How it was made
---------------
Developed by bloeiende with AI assistance (Claude). Game design, testing and
direction by bloeiende; most of the code was written with the AI.

--------------------------------------

- Optional: Trinkets (apron rules with accessories), Xaero's Minimap (waypoints)
